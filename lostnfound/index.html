Questions

How long do you keep items?

Unclaimed items are kept for 60 days, then donated or disposed of.

Can someone pick up for me?

Yes, with a signed note from you and a copy of your ID.

Why can’t I see all the details?

Serial numbers, names on IDs, and contents are hidden so only the real owner can describe them.

My item isn’t listed.

It may not be logged yet. Send a lost-item report and check back in a few days.

ACT Lost and Found. Items are returned in person only, with valid ID.
